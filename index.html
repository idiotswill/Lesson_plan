Privacy & connections
No analytics, accounts, passwords or AI calls. Python exercises download a pinned Pyodide runtime from jsDelivr, then execute in your browser. The CDN receives ordinary asset requests; we do not upload your code or progress. An internet connection is required for that runtime download; offline Python is not guaranteed.
